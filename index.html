My girl,
I don't think I tell you enough how much I appreciate you. Not just the cute things you do, not just the moments when you're being sweet with me, but you. The person you are, even on the days when you don't feel like your best self. I hope you know that you never have to be perfect around me. You don't have to always be happy, always have the right words, or pretend that everything is okay. I want the real you: the excited you, the sleepy you, the quiet you, the overthinking you, and even the you who sometimes doesn't know what she's feeling. You don't become any less precious to me on those days. I appreciate the little things more than you probably realise. The way you make ordinary conversations feel special, the way you make me smile at my phone, the random things you tell me, your silly moments, your softness, and all those tiny details that make you exactly who you are. Sometimes I catch myself smiling because I remembered something you said, and honestly, I hope I never stop having those little moments. I wish you could borrow my eyes for a minute and see how much there is to love about you. I wish you could be a little kinder to yourself, especially when you're doubting yourself. You deserve patience, gentleness, reassurance, and a love that doesn't make you feel like you have to earn your place. You deserve to feel appreciated without having to ask for it. Thank you for letting me be close to you. Thank you for trusting me with your thoughts, your feelings, your laughter, and the parts of you that not everyone gets to see. I don't take that for granted. I may not always find the perfect words, and I might not get everything right every time, but I want to keep learning how to love you better, listen better, and make you feel as valued as you truly are. And if one day gets heavy, if you feel tired, insecure, or simply not enough, please come back to this little message. You are not a burden. You are not difficult to care about. You are not someone I only want around when things are easy. You matter to me on the quiet days too. Especially then. I hope life gives you a thousand reasons to smile. I hope you keep growing into the person you want to be, without ever feeling pressured to change the beautiful parts of yourself just to fit someone else's expectations. And I hope I get to be there, cheering for you, being proud of you, laughing with you, and reminding you that you are loved. This isn't just an 11/11 message. I don't need a date on the calendar to be grateful that you exist. I just wanted to make something that you could open whenever you need a reminder that there's someone out here who thinks the world of you. So, one last time: thank you for being you. I'm so, so glad I get to know you, care about you, and call you mine. Please never forget how special you are to me.
I love you, always. ♡
